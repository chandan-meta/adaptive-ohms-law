## Performance and limits

The 4:3 run measured per-scene Editor mean frame times of 17.2–18.7 ms and p95 values of 17.4–18.0 ms. These are observations from a shared GPU with another Unity workload, not an isolated player benchmark. The recorded final run has additional readback/encoding overhead and a fixed 30 fps game clock; its timing must not be used as normal gameplay performance.

Material variants enable instancing. Added wire background lights cast no shadows; the wire shader uses probe reflections without screen refraction. The existing URP additional-light shadow-atlas capacity warning was present during development and remains a quality/performance limitation. No controlled before/after player benchmark was completed, so no hardware-independent performance or no-regression claim is made.

Physical touchscreen/mobile builds, microphone/network AI services, and all combinations of settings on every chapter were not certified. The Ask test used the project's fallback answer, not a verified remote AI session. Audio uses existing clips, routing and ducking; no new composed soundtrack is claimed.

## Media verification

The final MP4 contains 1920 × 1080 H.264 video (556.766667 seconds) and AAC audio (556.757 seconds), totaling 140,627,911 bytes. FFmpeg decoded the audio successfully; mean level was -24.4 dBFS and peak -1.6 dBFS. Sampled encoded frames were inspected through the finale. This confirms a non-silent, decodable mix and matching duration; it is not a subjective listening certification on multiple playback devices.

## Windows build and startup

Windows x64 release build succeeded with zero errors; Unity reported 262,592,394 bytes. Launch `Builds/CinematicPolish/OhmsLaw.exe` with its accompanying data and runtime folders intact.

The built player launched as a hidden smoke-test process, initialized Direct3D 12 on the NVIDIA RTX 5060 Ti, loaded assemblies, physics and input, and remained responsive. Its log contained no managed exceptions, missing references, or shader compilation errors. It was then closed. This is a startup smoke test, not a full manual playthrough of the standalone executable; complete playthroughs were performed in the Editor.

Existing warnings include SubtitleUI's hidden `enabled` member, an unused SceneDirector field, and the URP shadow-atlas capacity warning. The player also logged a D3D12 debug info-queue query failure, then successfully initialized the graphics device. These are recorded rather than characterized as a warning-free build.

Evidence: `Logs/CinematicPolish/build.txt`, `build-details.txt`, `player-smoke.log`, and `player-smoke-result.txt`.
