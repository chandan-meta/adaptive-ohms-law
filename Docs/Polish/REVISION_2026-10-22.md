# Ohm's Law module revision, 2026-10-22

The user's brief (Watson-led module: immersive audio, teaching order, one-click assembly, purposeful gestures, centred
table and graph, a memorable Ohm's law reveal, brief adaptive checks, an Explore Now lab, no longer than now) applied to
the existing module. This file is the contract between the work packages: decisions, file ownership, APIs, cue verbs and
the narration changes. Code comments follow the house style (why, not what).

## Decisions

1. **"Copper and mercury"** in the brief is the module's copper-vs-nichrome comparison (Scene 12). No mercury comparison
   exists; mercury appears only as Ohm's mercury connection cups (Scenes 7-8).
2. **Graph axes stay V vertical, I horizontal** (the NCERT V-I graph the module follows). The slope is ΔV/ΔI = R, and the
   module now says so with units, plus a one-line note that an I-against-V plot would have slope 1/R.
3. **Scenes 5 and 6 are never rebuilt** (the user hand-edits them). Their changes are runtime code, plus surgery on their
   generated Timeline assets only (`Editor/RevisionPatch.cs`): Scene 5 loses S05_V10 + S05_L09 (the repeated "how are they
   related? / want to predict?" prompts) and everything after them moves up; Scene 6 loses its `music off` / `ambience off`
   cues so the wire bed carries into Scene 7 and crossfades there. The .unity files are not touched.
4. **Two MCQs, both graded, framed as detective challenges.** Q1 = Scene 6's Detective Test (V up, R constant: current?),
   right after V and I. Q2 = Scene 12, after resistance is defined (R up, V fixed: current?). Correct: a check mark, a chime,
   Wattson's fist pump, one short line, continue. Wrong: the option is marked and disabled, a targeted hint (text plus a
   visual cue in the scene), retry at once, no extra narration; a second miss shows the answer and moves on (never trapped).
5. **Removed for pacing / repetition:** S05_V10, S05_L09; S09_L09 + the S09_CloseKey tap (the circuit completes itself when
   the voltmeter lands); S09_L11 + the `reminder` card (it re-asked the question); S10_Predict3V + S10_After3V (a second
   V-I prediction); S12_R01-R04, S12_N01, S12_QC (long material explanation, replaced by three short lines that state the
   conditions); S13_N01 (replaced by three short lines that give the law, its conditions and its rearrangements).
6. **The data stage is centred on the screen** while it shows (the dock fades out under it), the table and graph enter one
   after the other, and Wattson stands beside the stage on the side nearest what he talks about, pointing at it, with a
   beam stub from his fingertip to the stage edge and a ring on the exact row, point or axis. He never stands over it.
7. **The finale is one centred surface (LawReveal):** the three pieces V, I, R arrive one by one with a snap; the learner
   taps them into V = I × R; OHM'S LAW lands with one restrained accent; the conditions line and I = V ÷ R, R = V ÷ I join;
   then the bench sweeps 1 → 5 V while the equation's numbers update live (R stays 5 Ω): the current rises in step.
8. **Explore Now** on the case-closed card opens a small lab on Scene 13's bench (voltage slider, live meters, table and
   graph, R = V ÷ I check with units, 0 V handled, a resistance control unlocked after a right answer, one optional
   challenge). Student-controlled, no narration.

## API contract

### WP-B (WattsonController / WattsonGuide)

- `WattsonController.Gesture` gains `Snap` **appended last** (int 11): a held gesture that snaps once at its start, then
  hangs relaxed.
- `public bool Snap()` on WattsonController: a layered finger snap (~0.5 s) on the free hand (the one not pointing),
  thumb-and-middle click, a small spark burst at the fingertips, a glow flash, `SFX_Wattson_Snap` through the audio
  library. Returns false and does nothing when he is off screen / inactive or a snap ran less than 0.8 s ago.
  `public const float SnapLead = 0.18f`: seconds from the call to the click (reveals land on the click).
- **Steady:** idle motion (bob, sway, idle roll, weight shift, leg swing, talking arm beats, nods) damps to a quiet level
  automatically while a line plays (`SubtitleTrack.CurrentLine`, `SceneDirector.Speaking`, voice loudness), while he points,
  and while WattsonGuide holds him; breathing and blinks stay. Eases in/out over ~0.4 s, never pops.
- Guide overrides on WattsonController: `public Transform guidePoint` (wins over the Timeline's pointTarget and makes the
  effective gesture Point) and `public Vector3? guideHome` (wins over home / path, not over the Ask spot).
- `WattsonGuide` (static facade + a MonoBehaviour on the module canvas, created on first use):
  - `WattsonGuide.Point(RectTransform target, Vector2 pivot = default /* (0.5,0.5) */, RectTransform beside = null)`:
    he flies beside `beside` (default: the top-most active surface among LessonPanel's stage / hero / LawReveal that
    contains the target, else the target itself) on the side nearest the target, keeping his whole figure outside
    `beside`'s screen rect, the dock (when shown) and the caption bar; his depth is his current one clamped 1.6-3.2 m from
    the camera; he points at the target's pivot point; a thin beam stub runs from his fingertip to `beside`'s edge and a
    ring marks the target (row: a bracket on its left/right edge; point: a ring; axis: an underline). Follows layout
    changes every frame (run after LessonPanel's LateUpdate).
  - `WattsonGuide.Stand(RectTransform beside, int side = 0)` (beside, no pointing; side -1 left, +1 right, 0 auto).
  - `WattsonGuide.Release()`: back to the Timeline's gestures and markers (he flies back). Called on every scene entry.
  - `WattsonGuide.Active`.

### WP-C (panel, data views, finale)

  `GraphUI.Point(int) / AxisTitle(char 'V'|'I') / PlotRect` → RectTransform; `LessonPanel.StageRect`, `LessonPanel.HeroRect`.

    `point.law`, `point.clear` → WattsonGuide (`point.clear` = Release).
